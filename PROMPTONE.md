# Market Analysis prompt Template
---
## What is the [DATA TYPE] of [group] that [DESIRED INFORMATION] in [TIME], provide data points, credible sources(preferably .gov, .org or well known sources) reinforcing the information and why they are credible: if none: say there are no credible sources, and arrange data into a [GRAPH TYPE]

**TASK:**
This prompt will get the AI to gather information, format that information into a graph and provide details on reliability as well as sources to confirm validity
**Context/Input:**
[List the placeholders the user needs to fill:]
- **[DATA TYPE]:** The data point desired - eg. High, low, average, etc.
- **[GROUP]:** The target of research
- **[DESIRED INFORMATION]:** what do you want to know about the group
- **[TIME]:** what is the time frame you want to know about - eg. current 1940 - now, etc
- **[GRAPH TYPE]:** What type of graph best suits your needs
---
**OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:**
**Format:**
- graph with added resources
**Constraints:**
- provide data points
- requires sources and their validity
- Specifications
**Tone/Style:**
This will get a fairly uninteresting writing style providing the information you need, resources for verification and a graph for better digestability
